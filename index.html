Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

Clear answers about the one-time SEO service and what happens after the project.

What exactly is a one-time SEO service?+

It is a focused SEO project that diagnoses your website, prioritizes problems, improves key areas and gives you a practical roadmap. It is designed to create clarity and strengthen the foundation without requiring an immediate monthly commitment.

Will you only provide a report?+

No. The preferred approach includes implementation of agreed high-priority items where access and platform conditions allow. When your internal team must make a change, we provide clear instructions and examples.

How long does the project take?+

The timeline depends on website size, platform, access and scope. A typical small or medium business website can often be completed within a few weeks after access and priorities are confirmed.

Can we continue with monthly SEO afterward?+

Yes. After the one-time project, you can implement the roadmap internally or continue with Cresto Digital for content, authority building, technical monitoring and ongoing optimization.

Do you work with businesses outside Sri Lanka?+

Yes. Cresto Digital supports businesses remotely across international markets, including service companies, SaaS, e-commerce and location-based businesses.
